## What's New in TalentFlow Marketplace V1.2

### 1. Pre-application Talent Discovery & Privacy Controls
- Candidates control their discoverability via `profileDiscoverable: Boolean @default(true)`. Non-discoverable profiles are strictly omitted from talent search, filters, and CRM pools.

### 2. Employer Saved Candidates & Favorites
- Bookmark candidates into an employer-scoped repository with instant `isFavorite` toggle and favorites filtering.

### 3. Custom Employer Talent Pools
- Create custom talent pools (e.g. "Senior React Developers") to organize talent before job openings occur.

### 4. Direct Invite-to-Apply Workflow
- Send job invitations directly to candidates with custom messages. Candidate receives in-app notifications and transactional invitation emails via Resend.

### 5. Candidate Job Invitations Portal & One-Click Application Conversion
- Candidates view received invitations at `/job-seeker/invitations`.
- Accepting an invitation creates an application and seamlessly enters the V1.1 Hiring Pipeline.

### 6. Security Hardening & Authorization
- Robust BOLA (Broken Object Level Authorization) and IDOR protection across pools, notes, tags, and invitations.
- Saving a candidate or adding to a talent pool gives organizational metadata access only; private resumes and contact details remain protected and accessible ONLY through legitimate job applications.
