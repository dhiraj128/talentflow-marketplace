## What's New in V1.3.0

### 1. Candidate & Employer Interview Management
- Support for interview types: `PHONE`, `VIDEO`, `IN_PERSON`, `TECHNICAL`, `HR`, `FINAL`.
- Full status lifecycle: `SCHEDULED`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, `NO_SHOW`, `RESCHEDULED`.
- Candidate Interview Center (`/job-seeker/interviews`) featuring Upcoming, Completed, and Cancelled tabs, meeting provider join links, and candidate preparation instructions.
- Employer Interview Center (`/employer/interviews`) featuring management table, filters, rescheduling, cancellations, and completed session logging.

### 2. Candidate Privacy Guard & Employer Private Evaluation
- **P0 Candidate Privacy Guard**: Endpoints `GET /interviews/candidate` and `GET /interviews/:id` strip all `notes`, `feedback`, and `feedbackList` objects. Candidate API calls NEVER return employer private evaluation notes.
- **Private Evaluation Modal**: Endpoint `POST /interviews/:id/feedback` allows employers to rate candidates (1-5), select recommendation (`STRONG_HIRE`, `HIRE`, `NEUTRAL`, `NO_HIRE`, `STRONG_NO_HIRE`), and enter candidate strengths, concerns, and private evaluation notes. Direct candidate access on this endpoint returns `403 Forbidden`.

### 3. Formal Job Offer Management System
- Complete offer lifecycle: `DRAFT`, `SENT`, `VIEWED`, `ACCEPTED`, `DECLINED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `EXPIRED`.
- Offer fields: Title, salary amount, currency, period (yearly/monthly/hourly), joining date, employment type, work location, welcome message/terms, expiration date, decline reason.
- Candidate Offer Center (`/job-seeker/offers`): Active, Accepted, Declined, and Expired tabs with Accept and Decline modal actions.

### 4. Transactional Offer Acceptance & Hiring Pipeline Integration
- **Candidate Offer Visibility**: Candidate endpoints exclude `DRAFT` offers (`status IN ['SENT', 'VIEWED', 'ACCEPTED', 'DECLINED', 'WITHDRAWN', 'EXPIRED']`).
- **Transactional Accept Offer**: Accepting an offer transactionally updates:
  1. `JobOffer` status $\rightarrow$ `ACCEPTED`
  2. `Application` status $\rightarrow$ `HIRED`
  3. Inserts `ApplicationStatusHistory` record (`OFFERED` $\rightarrow$ `HIRED`)
  4. Records `AuditLog` entry (`OFFER_ACCEPTED`)
  5. Dispatches notifications & Resend transactional emails to both candidate and employer
- **Pipeline Integration** (`/employer/pipeline`): Direct integration with `ScheduleInterviewDialog` on `INTERVIEWING` stage cards and `CreateOfferDialog` on `OFFERED` stage cards.
