# TalentFlow Marketplace V1.3 — Interview & Offer Management System Documentation

## Overview
TalentFlow V1.3 expands the hiring pipeline with full-lifecycle **Interview Management** and **Offer Management** capabilities, fully integrated with PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, Audit Logging, Notification Center, Resend Transactional Email, and JWT/RBAC security guards.

---

## 1. Key Features Implemented

### A. Interview Extensions & Private Feedback
- **Interview Types**: `PHONE`, `VIDEO`, `IN_PERSON`, `TECHNICAL`, `HR`, `FINAL`.
- **Status Support**: `SCHEDULED`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, `NO_SHOW`, `RESCHEDULED`.
- **Location & Instructions**: Direct support for custom meeting links or physical location instructions.
- **Candidate Privacy Guard**: Candidate endpoints (`GET /interviews/candidate`, `GET /interviews/:id`) strictly strip all `notes`, `feedback`, and `feedbackList` objects. Candidates can NEVER retrieve employer private evaluation notes.
- **Employer Private Feedback**: Endpoint `POST /interviews/:id/feedback` enables employers to log overall ratings (1-5), recommendation (`STRONG_HIRE`, `HIRE`, `NEUTRAL`, `NO_HIRE`, `STRONG_NO_HIRE`), strengths, concerns, and private evaluation notes.

### B. Offer Management System
- **Offer Lifecycle**: `DRAFT`, `SENT`, `VIEWED`, `ACCEPTED`, `DECLINED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `EXPIRED`.
- **Offer Fields**: Title, salary amount, currency, period (yearly/monthly/hourly), joining date, employment type, work location, welcome message/terms, expiration date, decline reason.
- **Candidate Ownership**: Candidate endpoint (`GET /offers/candidate`) excludes `DRAFT` offers.
- **Transactional Acceptance**: Accepting an offer transactionally updates:
  1. `JobOffer` status $\rightarrow$ `ACCEPTED`
  2. `Application` status $\rightarrow$ `HIRED`
  3. Inserts `ApplicationStatusHistory` record (`OFFERED` $\rightarrow$ `HIRED`)
  4. Records `AuditLog` entry
  5. Dispatches notifications & emails to candidate and employer

### C. Portal & Integration Extensions
- **Candidate Offers Portal** (`/job-seeker/offers`): Tabs for Active, Accepted, Declined, and Expired offers with Accept/Decline modal actions.
- **Candidate Interviews Portal** (`/job-seeker/interviews`): View upcoming, completed, and cancelled interview sessions with video join buttons and candidate preparation instructions.
- **Employer Interviews Portal** (`/employer/interviews`): Complete scheduling, filtering, reschedule/cancel/no-show actions, and private evaluation feedback modals.
- **Employer Pipeline Integration** (`/employer/pipeline`): Schedule interview directly from candidate cards in `INTERVIEWING` stage; Extend formal job offers via `CreateOfferDialog` in `OFFERED` stage.
